# 🌍 Global Center for Responsible AI

Welcome to the Global Center for Responsible AI — an open learning and advocacy platform promoting ethical, transparent, and human-centered use of artificial intelligence.

We exist to ensure AI benefits people, protects rights, and builds trust in society.

---

### ✅ What we do:
✔ Public education and awareness  
✔ Responsible AI guidelines and resources  
✔ Research and case studies  
✔ Advocacy for ethical and accountable AI systems  
✔ Focus on global health, development, and societal impact
