# About the Global Center for Responsible AI

The Global Center for Responsible AI is an independent platform committed to promoting safe, ethical, and human-centered use of artificial intelligence.

Our mission is to ensure AI systems are transparent, fair, accountable, and aligned with human rights.

---

### Areas of Focus
- AI Ethics Education
- Responsible AI in Healthcare & Public Health
- Data Protection & Privacy
- Governance & Regulation
- Fairness and Bias Mitigation
- Open-source advocacy

We publish articles, research, toolkits, community resources, and policy guides to help both technical and non-technical communities understand and use AI responsibly.
