# What is Responsible AI?

Artificial Intelligence is transforming healthcare, finance, education, and nearly every sector of society. But with great power comes responsibility.

**Responsible AI** means designing, developing, and deploying AI in a way that is:
✅ Safe  
✅ Transparent  
✅ Accountable  
✅ Inclusive  
✅ Aligned with human rights

## Why it matters
- AI can discriminate if trained on biased data
- Automated decisions can be unfair or harmful
- Black-box systems reduce trust and transparency

## Principles of Responsible AI
1. **Fairness**
2. **Accountability**
3. **Transparency**
4. **Privacy and Security**
5. **Human Oversight**

Responsible AI is not just a technical issue — it’s ethical, legal, and societal. The goal is simple: AI should benefit people, not harm them.
